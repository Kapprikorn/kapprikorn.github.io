Privacy Policy – Color Blocks 2

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Color Blocks 2 ("the game") is a free game made by Kaprikorn as a hobby project. The game contains no ads and no in-app purchases. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Information we collect

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous gameplay statistics. We use them only to see how the game plays, so we can make the difficulty and balance better. The statistics include:

  • run results, such as your score and the level you reached;
  • run length, such as how long a run lasted and how many moves it took;
  • technical information that Firebase Analytics collects automatically: a random anonymous app-instance ID, your device model, Android version, app version and your country or region.

This information is not linked to your name, email address or any account. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, and does not ask for your location, contacts or photos. Because the statistics are anonymous, we cannot look up the data of one specific person.

How the information is used

The statistics are only used to balance and improve the game. We do not sell them, we do not use them for advertising, and the game shows no ads.

Google as a service provider

The statistics are processed by Google on our behalf, as described in Firebase's privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy. The game declares the Android internet permission so it can send these statistics.

Your choices

The game has no setting to switch the statistics off. If you do not want them to be sent, you can uninstall the game. If you have questions, you can contact us (see below).

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your device so you can continue where you left off. Uninstalling the game removes this data.

Third-party services

The game is distributed through app stores such as Google Play. These platforms may collect information as described in their own privacy policies. We have no access to, or control over, that information.

Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, you can email the developer at kaprikorn.gamify@gmail.com.
