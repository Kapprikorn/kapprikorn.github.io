Privacy Policy – Cauldron Cascade

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Cauldron Cascade ("the game") is a free game made by Kapprikorn as a hobby project. The game contains no ads and no in-app purchases. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no tracking and no analytics run by us.

Data stored on your device

The game may save things like your progress, scores and settings locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Third-party services

The game is distributed through app stores such as Google Play. These platforms may collect information as described in their own privacy policies, for example the Google Privacy Policy. We have no access to, or control over, that information.

Children's privacy

Because the game does not collect any personal information, it does not collect personal information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, you can email the developer at kaprikorn.gamify@gmail.com.
