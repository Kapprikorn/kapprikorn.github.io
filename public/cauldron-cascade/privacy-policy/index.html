Privacy Policy – Cauldron Cascade

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Cauldron Cascade ("the game") is a free game made by Kapprikorn as a hobby project. The game contains no ads and no in-app purchases. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Information we collect

We don't collect any personal information ourselves. The game has no ads, no analytics and no accounts of its own. The only exception is the optional Google Play Games sign-in described below, which is handled by Google.

Data stored on your device

The game stores your progress and settings on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, your Play Games player ID, display name and best scores are processed by Google Play Games Services under Google's Privacy Policy, so that the weekly and monthly leaderboards can work. Signing in is optional and the game is fully playable without it. The game itself collects no other personal data and shows no ads.

You can sign out of Google Play Games at any time, and you can ask Google to delete your Play Games data through your Google account.

Internet permission

The game declares the Android internet permission so that Google Play Games can work. The game does not use the internet for anything else, such as advertising or analytics.

Third-party services

The game is distributed through app stores such as Google Play. These platforms may collect information as described in their own privacy policies, for example the Google Privacy Policy. We have no access to, or control over, that information.

Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, you can email the developer at kaprikorn.gamify@gmail.com.
