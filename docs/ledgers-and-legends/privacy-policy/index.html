Privacy policy: Ledgers & Legends

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Ledgers & Legends is a free single-player game for Android made by Kaprikorn, a hobby developer. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no sign-in.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It has no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising code.

The only internet use: the clock

Quests, crafts and sales run in real time, also while the game is closed. So that changing the phone's clock can't skip those timers, the game asks a public time service for the current time:

  • When: when the game starts or comes back to the screen (and it retries now and then while it can't reach the service).
  • To: timeapi.io (a public time service, https://timeapi.io/api/time/current/zone?timeZone=UTC).
  • What is sent: only a request for the current UTC time. No account, device ID, game data or other information is sent with it.
  • What the time service sees: like any website, it receives your device's internet (IP) address to send its answer back. The game itself does not store or use your IP address. See timeapi.io for how they handle server logs.

Without internet the game still opens; it shows that it is offline and pauses its timers until it can check the time again.

Your game save

Your progress is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage, deletes it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
